Ovienmhada (2024) Weighting Comparison

Ovienmhada et al. (2024) weight vulnerability at double the share of hazard and exposure (additive 25/25/50). Both maps use current-period scores normalized 0–100 on a shared continuous scale. All three Spearman correlations across weighting schemes are ≥ 0.91 (A vs B = 0.911, A vs C = 0.942, B vs C = 0.936) and the top 5 facilities are stable, but individual facility ranks shift — most notably CHCF (rank 22 → 11) and ISP (rank 31 → 20).

VCP Comparison & Weighting Sensitivity

VCP's ExHeatHealth_Idx uses community-facing demographics that do not describe incarcerated populations (own-tract Spearman r = 0.072, community r = −0.214 vs our index). Max rank swing shown across all three weighting schemes; CHCF (swing=11) and ISP (swing=11) are the most sensitive facilities.
