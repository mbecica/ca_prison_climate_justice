Data note: The CDCR 2025 Climate Report shows 19% mechanical cooling system-wide, compared to 29% here. The source of this gap is not fully reconcilable from available data. The Reuters FOIA was scoped to air handling units (AHUs) at state prisons; CDCR's report includes a separate "fans only" category (5%) and may use a different unit of aggregation or a broader facility universe (e.g., fire camps, community facilities). A limitation of this analysis is that bed counts per housing unit within each facility are not publicly available — CDCR publishes population only at the institution level. Per-facility bars therefore show the share of housing units (not beds) with each cooling type, and population weighting uses facility-level totals as a proxy.

Skarha et al. (2023) found a 5.2% increase in total in-custody mortality on days where outdoor daily maximum temperature exceeds the facility's mean summer (Jun–Aug) tmax by 10°F or more. Near-term projection using avg 2016–2025 Skarha threshold days and a historic baseline mortality rate. Baseline: 2016–2019 all-cause mortality avg (3.07/1,000/year), matching the start of the heat data, pre-COVID and pre-fentanyl surge. Source: CCHCS Annual Mortality Review Reports, cchcs.ca.gov/reports/. Recent rates (4.0–4.4/1,000 in 2022–2024) are elevated by drug overdose deaths and are not used as the baseline to avoid conflating heat mortality with unrelated causes. Continuous slope adjustment: Skarha's 5.2% is the effect per 10°F above the facility mean summer tmax as a continuous variable, not a flat effect at the binary threshold. Across 2,988 facility-days above the Skarha threshold (2016–2025), the mean excess was 3.11°F above threshold (i.e., 13.11°F above facility mean), giving a mean slope weight of 1.31×. Person-days adjusted accordingly (840,457 × 1.31 = 1,102,039 slope-adjusted person-days). A binary threshold calculation would give 0.37 deaths/year. Non-refrigeration flag: Because Skarha pools AC and non-AC facilities, the 5.2% estimate is attenuated by mechanical cooling. Restricting to the 72.7% of person-days at facilities without refrigeration (evaporative or ventilation only) gives 611,051 person-days/year (801,488 slope-adjusted), yielding 0.35 deaths/year — 1 death every 2.9 years. This is a lower bound on the non-AC effect, as partial-AC facilities are counted proportionally by their non-ref unit share. Skarha, J., Jackson, A., Zlotnik, H., Williams, B., & Wildeman, C. (2023). Heat and Mortality in US State Prisons. PLOS ONE, 18(3):e0281389.
