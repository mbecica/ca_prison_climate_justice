CDCR Violent Incidents Extraction Notes
============================================================

VIOLENT CATEGORIES TRACKED SEPARATELY (per-facility Total rows):
  - Assault on a Peace Officer or Non-Prisoner (Total)  ->  assault_on_officer
  - Battery on a Peace Officer or Non-Prisoner (Total)  ->  battery_on_officer
  - Assault on Inmate (Total)  ->  assault_on_inmate
  - Battery on Inmate (Total)  ->  battery_on_inmate
  - Cell Extractions (Total)  ->  cell_extractions
  - Fighting  ->  fighting
  - Riot  ->  riot

DERIVED COLUMNS (computed at write time):
  - inmate_on_inmate = assault_on_inmate + battery_on_inmate + fighting + riot
  - staff_involved   = assault_on_officer + battery_on_officer + cell_extractions
  - violent_incidents = inmate_on_inmate + staff_involved  (backwards-compat total)

EXCLUSIONS:
  - All Controlled Substance rows
  - All Type of Force rows
  - 'Riot - Number of Inmates Involved' row (counts individuals, not incidents)
  - All other non-violent incident categories

METHOD NOTES:
  - Each facility spans 2-3 pages per PDF; all pages accumulated before storing.
  - Pages are grouped by (facility, month_column_set) to handle 2-3 page splits.
  - 'Riot' row matched exactly to avoid confusion with 'Riot - Number of Inmates'.
